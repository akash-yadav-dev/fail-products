# Code of Conduct

FailProducts is a community project about product failure, but failure is not an excuse for personal abuse.

We expect contributors and users to:

- criticize ideas and products rather than people;
- keep discussions constructive;
- avoid harassment, threats, hate, and doxxing;
- respect privacy;
- accept good-faith disagreement;
- report serious problems privately when appropriate.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct harms the safety or usefulness of the community.
