The FailProducts source code is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only**. See [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE)
and [`NOTICE.md`](./NOTICE.md).

The **name "FailProducts", the FailProducts logo, wordmark, and visual identity are not
covered by that licence.** They are reserved by the project maintainer, Akash Yadav.

This is deliberate. The AGPL exists to guarantee that anyone can run, study, modify, and
self-host this software. A trademark reservation exists for a different reason: so that a
person arriving at a site can tell whether they are looking at the official FailProducts
directory or somebody's fork. Those two goals do not conflict — the code stays free, the
identity stays distinguishable.

## You may

- use, modify, self-host, and distribute the software under the AGPL-3.0-only terms
- run a public instance of your modified version
- state accurately that your project is "based on FailProducts", "a fork of FailProducts",
  or "powered by FailProducts source code"
- reference the name in documentation, articles, comparisons, and academic work
- link to failproducts.com

## You may not

- name your instance, fork, product, or service "FailProducts", or a name likely to be
  confused with it
- use the FailProducts logo, wordmark, or visual identity as the branding of your instance
- register a domain, social account, package name, or app-store listing that presents itself
  as FailProducts
- imply endorsement, affiliation, or official status without written permission
- use the marks in a way that suggests your instance is the canonical directory

## If you fork

Replace the name, logo, and wordmark with your own before publishing your instance. A short
attribution line in your footer or README — "Based on FailProducts" with a link — is welcome
and sufficient.
